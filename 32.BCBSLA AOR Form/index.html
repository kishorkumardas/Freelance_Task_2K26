Healthcare Provider as Authorized Representative Form

Why Do I Need a Healthcare Provider as Authorized Representative Form? This form is to give someone like a healthcare provider permission to appeal a coverage decision for you.

Information about the Service:

Step 1: Member Authorization

I give authorization to the healthcare provider named on this form to begin the first and/or second level of appeal regarding the benefit determination on the claim(s) described on this form.

I authorize Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Louisiana to release any of my protected health information to the healthcare provider named on this form for the purpose of resolving my appeal. This authorization will expire upon resolution of my appeal.

Step 2: Healthcare Provider as Authorized Representative

As the healthcare provider and authorized representative for the above-named member, I/we/it waive(s) any right to payment from the above-named member other than any applicable copayment or other coinsurance amount based on the member's plan.

You or your representative may return this letter and the completed form with your appeal to:
