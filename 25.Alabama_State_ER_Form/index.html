Section 4: Claims for urgent care

If the situation is urgent, you may ask for an expedited review. An urgent care situation is one in which the covered person's health may be in serious jeopardy or, in a doctor's opinion, the covered person may have pain that cannot be controlled while waiting for the external review decision.

Alabama Department of Insurance note: External review is separate from filing a consumer complaint.
