Read before submitting. In most cases, you must complete the mandatory internal appeal offered by the health plan or insurance issuer before an external review can begin. The completed request generally must be received within four months of the date the plan or insurer sent the final decision denying services or payment.

Alabama note: Alabama external review requests are generally handled through the HHS-administered Federal External Review Process. Submit to MAXIMUS Federal Services by fax at 1-888-866-6190 or by mail to HHS Federal External Review Request, MAXIMUS Federal Services, 3750 Monroe Avenue, Suite 708, Pittsford, NY 14534. Use this form as an Alabama-specific working version of the federal external review request.
